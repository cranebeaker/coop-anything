# Resident Evil 2 (2019 remake): online campaign co-op research

## Scope and research date

This entry concerns online campaign co-op between separate PCs, not local
shared-screen or split-screen play. The research date is **2026-10-23**; this
cleanup does not represent a new search or advance the directory's checked date.

No released online campaign co-op project was recorded in the sources searched
on that date. This is a limited, dated search result, not a claim that no private
or unreleased prototype exists.

## Existing local projects

- [Local Couch Co-op / HUNK's Friend](https://www.nexusmods.com/residentevil22019/mods/2938):
  local co-op using REFramework. It has a separate project entry in the directory.
- [RE2 Party Mode](https://www.nexusmods.com/residentevil22019/mods/3024):
  local split-screen project, also listed separately.

Local multiplayer is not evidence of online campaign synchronization. These
projects are relevant findings, not online releases filling the gap.

## Search coverage and limitations

The recorded research covered GitHub repository search, the Nexus Mods pages and
posts above, ModDB and general web search. Detailed query logs were not retained.
Steam forums and Reddit were not independently rechecked for this entry. The
directory's `searched` list records this coverage and its limitations explicitly.

## Evidence boundary

This PR contains research only: **no game tests or playable online mod are supplied**.
Protocol simulations, fuzz session counts and tests of isolated features do not
establish synchronized game objects, story progression or a playable campaign.
They are not used as evidence for this gap entry. Its QA level remains `none`.
